# Storm Disasters

A local s&box add-on targeting stock Sandbox Mode. From the repository root on NixOS, run `./launch.sh --addon` to refresh the shared tornado assets and open the add-on. Otherwise, run `node tools/sync-storm-disasters.cjs` from the repository root and open `Addons/StormDisasters/storm_disasters.sbproj` in s&box. Play Sandbox’s `scenes/sandbox.scene` to play on stock Flatgrass.

Aim at open ground, then open **Q → Entities → Tornadoes** to spawn a preset. The presets are listed only under Entities. **Cone**, **Cylinder**, **Hourglass**, **Rope**, **V-shaped**, and **Wedge** each have six separately spawnable entries, **F0–F5**. Higher ratings make that shape taller and wider. Each tornado follows a curving, variable-speed path and stays near the ground. Press **Z** to undo the most recent spawn. Sprites are the fallback if the funnel shader is unavailable. The add-on does not require the Storm Chasers map and is not published to the Workshop.

All tornado presets move nearby props simulated by the host. The wind follows the funnel's width and bend at each height: it rotates fastest around the vortex core, draws air inward near the ground, rises around the core, and weakens outward. The wedge has a comparatively calm eye. Solid static cover reduces local wind, and drag depends on the prop's speed relative to the air, its projected area, and its mass. A light crate is easier to lift than compact, heavy debris. Higher ratings add gameplay-tuned lift from wind pressure. Once actually lifted into the core of an F2 or stronger tornado, props can circle the moving funnel as they climb. After at least one and a half revolutions and enough height, the tornado throws them outward with their tangential momentum intact; gravity then takes over. F0 still mostly pushes props along the ground. The F0–F5 peak wind inputs (75, 98, 123, 150, 184, and 220 mph) are representative values drawn from the [National Weather Service's EF bands](https://www.weather.gov/oun/efscale), not prop speeds or measured gusts. This is an approximate vortex and drag model, not a fluid or structural-damage simulation.

**F0–F2** never unfreeze props. In strong enough local wind, **F3**, **F4**, and **F5** roll on first contact for each frozen prop, then retry if it is still frozen and in range: **F3: 20% every 20 seconds; F4: 40% every 15 seconds; F5: 65% every 10 seconds**. These odds are a Sandbox gameplay rule, not a meteorological claim. Map geometry, static props, and players receive no direct wind forces or damage; ordinary Sandbox prop collisions still apply. `Tests/PropWindSmoke.cs.txt` checks all ratings, `Tests/PropWindFieldSmoke.cs.txt` checks shape, shelter and drag, `Tests/PropWindLoftSmoke.cs.txt` checks the climbing orbit and release, and `Tests/PropWindCooldownSmoke.cs.txt` checks retries. None touches your undo stack.

The tornado snaps to solid ground before appearing. Dust starts spinning at the surface while the condensation funnel descends from the wall cloud over about twelve seconds, easing out of the cloud and slowing as it meets the dust. The funnel bends as it travels and fades out without lifting off. Its wall cloud overlaps the shared low overcast; the visual reach does not change the prop-wind profile. The foot blends into rotating, rising sand-colored dust rendered inside the funnel volume, without a separate smoke overlay. Wedges sweep a skirt of dust along the ground; near the strongest wedges, gusts make rain streaks longer and more horizontal and strengthen the wind sound. The eye stays comparatively calm. F0 keeps a light plume; stronger ratings loft faster, denser, more sharply defined rolls instead of just scaling up the same dust. While at least one tornado is active, the map gets shared overcast, wind-driven rain, and a mix of wind, rain, covered rain, and a spatial tornado roar. Rain is suppressed under cover. The weather stops when the last tornado is removed or expires. Fog, automatic spawning, intensity upgrades, map-specific routes, and ground scarring are not part of the add-on yet. Storm Chasers does not depend on Storm Disasters yet.

The generator copies the game’s funnel and debris shaders, bakes the standalone funnel-density texture, adapts the standalone debris animation, and reads the F0–F5 profile heights and widths, shape radii, and wedge eye radius. The walk-in wedge uses the game's wedge dimensions at F5 and scales them down for lower ratings. Source templates and asset generators live in `tools/storm-disasters/`, including `Weather.csx.in` for runtime weather and `weather-assets.cjs` for the baked masks and audio. Edit these sources rather than the generated assets. Regenerate the add-on files before committing a shared change, and sync before publishing the separately versioned add-on.

From the repository root, `node --test Tests/StormDisastersSync.test.cjs` checks synchronization and `node Addons/StormDisasters/Tests/check-layout.cjs` validates all 36 entries. On Flatgrass, run `Tests/SpawnlistSmoke.cs.txt` through the editor's `run_csharp` tool to verify that all 36 presets qualify for the native Entities category and the add-on's Spawnlists entry is absent. `Tests/FormationSmoke.cs.txt` checks the descending tip and early ground dust, `Tests/VolumeSmoke.cs.txt` checks all six shapes, and `Tests/GroundContactSmoke.cs.txt` checks elevated spawns, grounded travel, and rejection when no support exists. None touches the player's undo stack. In your own Play session, run `Tests/FlatgrassSpawnSmoke.cs.txt` to exercise stock spawn and undo for the original three presets. Do not run that test while someone else is spawning items in the same session.
